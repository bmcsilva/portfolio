About me

I started my professional journey in 2016, working in production and technical support roles. Over time I built a strong foundation in hardware, support, and manual testing — skills that later made me a better developer.

In 2018 I stepped into the role of Manual Software Tester, sharpening my eye for detail and learning to communicate clearly with development teams.
